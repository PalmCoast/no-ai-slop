How the math works

The planner runs these steps, in this order, on the numbers you type.

  1. Creatives live at once. Ad sets × creatives per ad set.
  2. Refreshes per month. 52 ÷ 12 ÷ your refresh weeks (52 weeks spread over 12 months).
  3. New creatives per month. Creatives live at once × refreshes per month, rounded up to a whole creative.
  4. Cost per month. New creatives per month × your cost per creative. Per year is both numbers × 12.
  5. AI Ad Sprint next to it. New creatives per month ÷ 12 ads per sprint, rounded up, × $297. One sprint works out to $24.75 an ad ($297 ÷ 12).

Worked example (the example numbers): 3 ad sets × 4 creatives = 12 live. Refreshing every 4 weeks is about 1.08 refreshes a month. 12 × 1.08 = 13 new creatives a month (rounded up). At $100 each, that's $1,300 a month, or 156 creatives and $15,600 a year. 13 ÷ 12 rounded up is 2 sprints, which is $594.

Quick answers

Answers updated November 1, 2026.

How many creatives per ad set should I run?

There's no single right number. Run as many genuinely different ideas as that ad set's budget can give a fair read, which for a small budget often means a few strong concepts, not dozens of near-copies. Check your ad platform's current guidance, then plug your number into the planner.

How often should I refresh my ad creatives?

Refresh when an ad's results start slipping as the same people keep seeing it, not on a fixed calendar alone. The planner's "refresh every" field lets you plan around your own pace.

What is creative fatigue?

Creative fatigue is when your audience has seen an ad so many times that it stops getting the response it used to. Fresh hooks and new angles are the usual fix.

How much do ad creatives cost?

It depends on who makes them and how, so the planner uses your own cost per creative instead of an average. For reference, our AI Ad Sprint is 12 finished ads for $297.

How many new ads do I need per month?

Multiply your ad sets by the creatives in each, then by how many times a month you refresh them. The planner does that math and shows each step.

Is the creative planner free?

Yes. It's free, with no sign-up, and the formula is shown on the page so you can check it.
