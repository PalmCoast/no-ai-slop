The best DTC ads don't look like a tool made them. They look like the brand: the same colors, the same voice, the product front and center. The tooling sits underneath. Here's how to make them, and the two ways we'd do it.

What a good DTC video ad has

  • The product in the first second. No logo intro. Show the thing.
  • Your brand's look. Your fonts, your colors and your photography style, so the ad feels like your site.
  • One message per ad. One benefit, one line, one offer.
  • Sizes for each feed. Vertical for stories and reels, square and 4:5 for feeds.
  • Enough versions to test. Several hooks for the same product, so the numbers pick the winner.
