Best Hosting for a Startup Website

Fast, reviewable, and ready for the whole team. What to look for, and the host we use.

Disclosure: some links here are affiliate links, marked as sponsored. If you sign up or buy, AgentHive Inc may earn a fee at no extra cost to you. We also sell some items on this page ourselves. How we earn.

A startup site has one job: look like the company is already the obvious choice. The host decides how fast that site loads, how quickly a change ships, and whether the team can review it before customers see it.

What to look for

  • Deploys from your repo. Every push builds and goes live with no manual upload.
  • Preview links. Each pull request gets its own URL so design, marketing and engineering can sign off.
  • Fast everywhere. A global CDN and HTTPS on your own domain by default.
  • Room for the team. Pricing that doesn't punish you for adding people.
  • Forms and functions built in. Signup forms and small backend jobs without a separate server.
