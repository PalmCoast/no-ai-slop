Is 32GB enough?

For games, yes. Of the top 15 Steam games we track, 13 recommend 16GB or less, 1 (Borderlands 4) recommends 32GB, and 1 lists only a minimum. See how much RAM games need in 2026 for the full list with links to each requirements page. If you edit video or run virtual machines as well, look at 64GB kits.

Spending less or going faster

The lowest 16GB DDR5 kit we verified cost $247.99 (Patriot Viper Venom DDR5 RAM 16GB (2X8GB) 6000MT/s CL36 at Amazon). For Intel Core Ultra 200S (Z890) boards, the 48GB DDR5-8000 kits started at $859.99; 8000 MT/s is above Intel's rated DDR5-6400, so check your board's memory support list first. Questions

What is the best 32GB DDR5 kit for the money?

On November 19, 2026, the CORSAIR Vengeance RS DDR5 32GB (2 x 16GB) 6000MHz CL38 (CML32GX5M2D6000Z38) was the lowest price we verified at $469.99 and had the tightest timings of the three 32GB kits (CL38). We rank by price and timings and have not benchmarked the kits.

Is DDR5-6000 CL30 worth it over CL38?

At 6000 MT/s, CL30 works out to about 10.0 ns first-word latency and CL38 to about 12.7 ns. On Nov 19, 2026 the CL30 kit was also the cheaper one, so there was no premium to pay for it.

Is 32GB enough for gaming in 2026?

For the top 15 Steam games we track, yes: 13 recommend 16GB or less and the rest recommend at most 32GB or list only a minimum.

Should I wait for Black Friday to buy RAM?

BottleneckPC's Black Friday guide (October 21, 2026) says no for RAM: it logged a 32GB DDR5-6000 kit at about $485 on Amazon, says the same capacity cost about $75 in early 2025, and advises buying the capacity you need when you need it (source).
