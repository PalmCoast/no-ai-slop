Questions

Should I wait for Black Friday to buy RAM?

BottleneckPC's Black Friday guide (October 21, 2026) says no for RAM: it logged a 32GB DDR5-6000 kit at about $485 on Amazon, says the same capacity cost about $75 in early 2025, and advises buying the capacity you need when you need it (source). This page shows the prices we could verify, with the date each one was checked, so you can judge for yourself.

Why is RAM so expensive in 2026?

AI data centers are buying a large share of the world's memory, and supply has not caught up. Tom's Hardware and The Verge covered the shortage in October 2026. We don't quote a single market number here; every price on this page links to its own source.

How much RAM do I need for gaming?

Check the game. Each game in the "Games to play with it" section shows the minimum and recommended RAM from its official requirements page, with a link. Pick your RAM size and the list sorts itself into games that meet the recommended amount, games that meet only the minimum, and games below the minimum.

Is DDR4 still worth buying?

If your motherboard takes DDR4, a DDR4 kit is the only kind that fits it, and DDR4 kits are listed here alongside DDR5. DDR4 and DDR5 are not interchangeable, so check which one your board uses before you buy.

Are these affiliate links?

Links marked "Sponsored" are affiliate links, and AgentHive Inc may earn a commission if you buy through one. Every other store link is a plain link. Being an affiliate link never changes a price we show or how the list is sorted.

How often are the prices checked?

Every price shows the date it was checked and a link to where it came from. Prices change often, especially around Black Friday, so confirm the price on the store's page before you buy.
