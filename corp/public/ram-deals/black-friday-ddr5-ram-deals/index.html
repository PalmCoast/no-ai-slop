Should I wait for Black Friday to buy RAM?

BottleneckPC's Black Friday guide (October 21, 2026) says no for RAM: it logged a 32GB DDR5-6000 kit at about $485 on Amazon, says the same capacity cost about $75 in early 2025, and advises buying the capacity you need when you need it (source). Every price here shows the date we checked it, so you can compare it with what you see on the day.

Are these Black Friday prices?

They are the prices the store pages showed when we checked them on November 19, 2026, signed out, for a US ZIP code. We list the price we saw, the seller and a link to the page. We do not predict Black Friday prices, and prices change often, so confirm on the store page before you buy.

Can I put DDR5 in a DDR4 motherboard?

No. DDR4 and DDR5 sticks have different notches and do not fit each other's slots. Look up your motherboard model (on Windows, System Information lists it as BaseBoard Product) on the maker's site to see which type it takes.
