Questions

Is 16GB of RAM enough for gaming in 2026?

For most games, yes: 13 of the top 15 Steam games we track recommend 16GB or less. Borderlands 4 recommends 32GB.

Is 32GB of RAM worth it for gaming?

32GB meets the recommended RAM of every top-15 game that lists one and leaves room for apps in the background. The lowest 32GB kits we verified on Nov 19, 2026 cost $229.99 for DDR4 and $469.99 for DDR5.

Do I need 64GB of RAM for gaming?

Not for the games themselves: none of the top 15 Steam games we track recommend more than 32GB. 64GB helps if you also stream, edit video or run local AI models.

Where do these RAM numbers come from?

Each game's minimum and recommended RAM is copied from the system requirements on its Steam store page, linked in the table with the date we checked it.

Can I put DDR5 in a DDR4 motherboard?

No. DDR4 and DDR5 sticks have different notches and do not fit each other's slots. Look up your motherboard model (on Windows, System Information lists it as BaseBoard Product) on the maker's site to see which type it takes.
