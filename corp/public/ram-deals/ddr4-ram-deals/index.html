Check before you buy

  • DDR4 or DDR5: they are not interchangeable. On Windows, open System Information and look up the BaseBoard Product on your motherboard maker's site.
  • Desktop or laptop: every kit here is a desktop DIMM. Laptops use the smaller SO-DIMM.
  • Replace, don't mix: a matched kit is the simplest upgrade. Mixing a new kit with old sticks can mean running at the slower kit's speed.
  • Slots and maximum: your board's manual lists how many slots it has and the most memory it supports.

Questions

What is the cheapest 32GB DDR4 kit right now?

On November 18, 2026 it was the Team T-Force Delta RGB 32GB (2 x 16GB) DDR4 3200 Desktop Memory (TF3D432G3200HC16FDC01) at $239.99 at Newegg.

Is DDR4 still worth buying in 2026?

If your motherboard takes DDR4, yes: it is the only type that fits, and it lets you add memory without replacing the board and CPU.

Can I put DDR5 in a DDR4 motherboard?

No. DDR4 and DDR5 sticks have different notches and do not fit each other's slots. Look up your motherboard model (on Windows, System Information lists it as BaseBoard Product) on the maker's site to see which type it takes.

Is 16GB of DDR4 enough for gaming?

For most current games, it meets the recommended amount: 13 of the top 15 Steam games we track recommend 16GB or less. 32GB leaves room for games that recommend more and for apps running in the background.
