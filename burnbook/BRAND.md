# Burn Book

A First Deploy AI product from AgentHive Inc, Palm Coast, Florida. Public host: `burnbook.firstdeploy.ai`.

## Name

Burn Book. Two words. The object is a ledger for one month of AI spend. The red mark on the cover is the cut.

## Mark

`public/mark.jpg` is the logo: a black cover, a red spine, three rules, the lowest rule broken by a short red cut. `public/favicon.svg` is the same mark for the tab. Do not redraw it as a chat bubble, a flame, or a gradient.

## Color

- Paper `#f3efe4`
- Ink `#1a1714`
- Cut `#8f2d2d`
- Keep `#1e4a38`

Type is Fraunces for the name and the price, Outfit for the interface, IBM Plex Mono for the figures.

## Voice

Say the dollars and the rule. The cuts are arithmetic on numbers the visitor typed. Market figures stay on the front page and the method page, with the publisher named, and they are not inputs to a customer's book.

## Price

$750 once, paid in full. The ledger is free. The packet is the memo, the letters, the twelve-month repeat of this month, and the files. The twelve-month line is this month times twelve. The page says it is not a prediction.

The $750 does not include someone making the cuts. That sentence stays on the offer.
