# Aisle

Paper catalog, not a chat window. Brass rules on a warm page. The two doors have their own rooms: Brown Wool is cream and brown, Flange Tube is aluminum gray and mono type.

The mark is three shelf lines. The word on a sample door is the shop name, with a way back to every aisle.

Copy stays on the spec. Say the size, the material, and the feature. Say which shop. Say the price on the shelf and that the buyer should open the shop to confirm stock.

Publish is $29 once. Do not describe it as a subscription.
