## The story (say this)

AskYard is a free desk. Anyone with a question about AI gets a plain answer. Then we offer to do the work.

The meter lets you search a name. If something rough is posted in public, you see it.

Marquee puts a name in lights. You type the dollar amount. Highest sits at #1. The next founder who wants it more buys the crown. You drop.

Founders would die for the name at the top.

- Stripe, Checkout, webhooks, secret keys, demo bids, or “it works without a card”
- Netlify, functions, blobs, edge rewrites, AI Gateway, model names, or API paths
- How the meter is scored, which sites it reads, or that any number is seeded
- Internal branches, PRs, labs, or “the site is not live yet”
- Other AgentHive products except First Deploy AI as the priced offer on the free answer

If someone asks how it works: “Search a name. Type a bid. Highest sits at one.” Then the URL.
