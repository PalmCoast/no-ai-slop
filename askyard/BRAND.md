# AskYard brand

## Name

AskYard is the yard where anyone can ask. Shop yard, school yard, equipment yard. The name is short enough to say over a truck radio and exact enough to live as `askyard.firstdeploy.ai`.

One accent. No extra colors pulled from the hero photograph. The photo is dusk and dust; the UI stays gold on black.

## Voice

Write like the shop talks.

- Lead with the job. "Stop missing night calls" not "unlock after-hours intelligence".
- Prices on the same screen as the answer.
- Marquee is vanity with a price they type. "Your name in lights. You name the bid."
- No banned words: leverage, seamless, empower, robust, cutting-edge, elevate, harness.
- No "it's not X, it's Y". Say the thing.

## Audience

Plumbers, teachers, receptionists, earth movers, and anyone else who will not sit through a demo. Free answer first. Offer second.
