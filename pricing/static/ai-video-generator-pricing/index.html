Higgsfield vs Runway vs Luma: what an AI video actually costs

Affiliate disclosure: The Higgsfield links on this page are Daniel Graham's affiliate links. If you subscribe through one, AgentHive Inc may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Runway and Luma links are plain links with no affiliate. Prices below were copied from each company's public pricing page on October 22, 2026 (re-checked October 23, 2026). Nobody paid for placement.

Short answer: all three sell monthly credits, and every model spends them at its own rate. Higgsfield starts at $19 a month for 270 credits. Runway's paid plans start at $12 a month billed yearly. Luma starts at $30 a month. The plan that's cheapest for you depends on which model you use and how many clips you finish each month.

Pick in one line

  • Higgsfield if you want lots of third-party models (Kling, Seedance, Nano Banana) in one account, plus ad and avatar tools.
  • Runway if you want its own Gen-4.5 models, an editor, and (on Max) credits that roll over one month.
  • Luma if you want its Ray models, including HDR output, and you're fine with a higher entry price.

What a credit buys, per each pricing page

  • Higgsfield: about 14 credits for an 8-second 720p Kling 3.0 clip, and 2 credits for a Nano Banana Pro image. The page estimates Starter covers about 15 Seedance 2.0 Fast videos.
  • Runway: 625 credits buys about 52 seconds of Gen-4.5 or 104 seconds of Gen-4 Turbo.
  • Luma: Ray3.2 text-to-video costs 100 credits per 5 seconds at 720p and 400 at 1080p. Seedance 2.0 costs 51 credits per second at 720p.

Rough math for a 720p, roughly 8-second clip on Kling 3.0 inside Higgsfield Plus: 1,200 ÷ 14 ≈ 85 clips a month. That's before retries. Most people throw away several takes for every clip they keep, so plan on fewer finished videos.

Watch for these before you pay

  • On Higgsfield, subscription credits reset every billing cycle and don't roll over.
  • Higgsfield's unlimited windows and free generations only work on higgsfield.ai. They don't work through its MCP/CLI, Canvas, or Supercomputer.
  • Higgsfield's Starter plan unlocks only some models. If you need a specific model, check it's included before buying.
  • Annual billing locks you in for a year. Try one month first.

FAQ

Is Higgsfield cheaper than Runway?

At the entry level, no. Runway Standard is $12–$15 and Higgsfield Starter is $19. At the top end, it depends on the model. Compare one specific clip type on both pricing pages instead of comparing credit counts.

Does Higgsfield have a free plan?

The October 23, 2026 pricing page lists only paid individual plans, Starter through Ultra, plus free generations on some Higgsfield tools. Check the page for current offers.

Which one should a founder making short social ads pick?

Start with the cheapest monthly plan on the tool whose model you like best, make ten real clips, then decide. Don't buy annual on day one.
