Install

  1. Open this page on the phone and tap Download.
  2. Open the file. If prompted, allow your browser to install unknown apps (a one‑time Android setting).
  3. Tap Install. It appears as Fold Companion.
  4. In Settings, set your four call buttons and your desktop agent's name.

Reminder

  • This app will not record calls, and has no internet permission.
  • The optional mail list is read‑only and stays on the device.
