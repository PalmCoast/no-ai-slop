Fold Companion

Private call & route-off-phone app for Daniel's Galaxy Z Fold 7.

Download the app (.apk)

On the Fold, scan this or open this page and tap Download.

Install

  1. Open this page on the Fold and tap Download the app.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If prompted, allow your browser to install unknown apps (one-time Android setting).
  3. Tap Install. It appears as Fold Companion.
  4. Optional: in the app's Mail tab, enable the notification listener yourself to see a local Gmail/Yahoo list.

What it will not do

  • This will not record calls. No background mic, no call audio, ever.
  • No internet permission — nothing leaves the phone unless you tap Export.
  • No reading SMS, passwords, or other apps' screens.
  • No accessibility clicking, overlays, or device admin.

Personal build. Not listed on any store.
